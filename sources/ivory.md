## Why Ivory is a good source

The megamenu is a labelled taxonomy of consumer tech written by Israelis for
Israelis. It is retail Hebrew rather than trade Hebrew — closer to catalogue
register than to what a technician says — which makes it most useful *paired*
with a trade source, where the gap between the two registers becomes visible.

## What it taught

- **Ivory's own naming is sometimes the formal end of the spectrum.** It lists
  `סוללת גיבוי ניידת` for a power bank; the street says פאוור בנק. Both are
  recorded, with the spoken form in `alternatives`. See `power-bank`.
- **Some Hebrew coinages have simply won.** אל פסק for a UPS is universal and has
  no English competitor in speech. See `ups`.
- **Retail groups categories differently from the English-speaking world.**
  תאורה ובית חכם puts lighting and home automation in one bucket; רב שקע חשמל
  (a mains power strip) sits under the same heading as USB and HDMI splitters.
  Knowing the grouping matters as much as knowing the word when navigating a site.
- **Hebrew frame + borrowed noun is the dominant pattern** for appliances:
  מכשיר למינציה, קורא כרטיסי זיכרון, מסך מחשב. The frame word (מכשיר, מכונה,
  קורא) is what makes the phrase searchable.
