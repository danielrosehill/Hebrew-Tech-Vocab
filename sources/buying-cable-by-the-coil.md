# Buying cable: coil vs. cut-to-length

**Researched:** 2026-07-29
**Trigger:** wanting ~100m of ordinary cable to make up short leads around the
flat, and finding only per-metre counter sales. "I hate buying stuff by the
metre. When I went into the store, they asked me how many metres."

No screenshot for this one — it started as a shop experience and was resolved by
reading vendor listings. Kept here because the finding is a vocabulary finding.

## The problem was the search term, not the market

Full coils are sold everywhere. They are simply a **separate product listing**
under a different word, and searching `כבל חשמל` never reaches them:

| Search term | What you get |
|---|---|
| `כבל חשמל` | finished appliance leads, and cut-to-length counter sales |
| `גליל` | the coil SKU — 50m or 100m, priced as one item |
| `חבילה` | same thing in manufacturers' catalogues |
| `תוף` | drum, for larger cross-sections |

`מטר רץ` is the header on the cut-to-length listing. That correction matters:
the dictionary previously guessed מטר רץ was confined to flooring and fabric.
It is not — Israeli cable listings use it as standard.

## The word that was actually missing

**כבל פנדל** — the white flexible 3-core PVC cable that leads are made from.
From German *Pendel*, via pendant-lamp flex. Media Galaxy's own copy names the
use case exactly: *מתאים להכנת כבל מאריך, כבלי חיבור למכשירים*.

Nothing about `כבל חשמל` or `כבל גמיש` leads you there, and Google Translate
cannot, because פנדל is a product category name rather than a translation of
anything.

| Item | Price | Per metre |

| כבל פנדל 3x1.5 white, **50m coil** (Media Galaxy) | ₪249 | ₪4.98 |
| כבל פנדל 3x1.5 white, **per running metre** (same vendor) | ₪6 | ₪6.00 |
| כבל ירוק XLPE/NYY 3x1.5, **100m pack** (Polack, Nisani) | ₪440–479 | ~₪4.50 |
| חוט קשיח 2.5mm single core, **100m** (Media Galaxy) | ₪165 | ₪1.65 |

The coil is roughly **17% cheaper per metre** than cutting to length. So the
instinct was right in both directions: buying to the nearest metre is the
expensive way, and it is avoidable.

## What to actually buy

For short custom-length leads indoors: **גליל 50 מטר כבל פנדל 3x1.5 לבן**,
about ₪249, plus תקעים. 50m is the stocked unit for pendel; the 100m coils are
mostly the green installation cable, which is stiff, outdoor-rated and the wrong
thing for flexible leads.

## Colour is the product name

Israeli suppliers identify cable by sheath colour and expect you to do the same.
From Amrusi's category description:

- **ירוק** — double insulated, fairly rigid, to 90°C, suitable outdoors
- **לבן** — flexible, to 70°C, indoors
- **כתום** — special insulation (fire-resistant; matches the חסין אש category
  seen on the cable supplier's grid)

Asking for "the green one" is precise trade speech, not vagueness. Marked
`confidence: medium` — one vendor's description, worth a second source.

## Vendors found

Online, all selling coils: mediagalaxy.co.il (Haifa), polack.co.il,
nisanihashmal.co.il, askol.co.il, erco.co.il, amrusi.co.il, ateka.co.il,
gardencenter.co.il. Hashmal Yashir (yashir-group.co.il) publishes a full 2025
catalogue as a PDF, which is the best single reference for what sizes exist and
whether each ships as a חבילה or a תוף.
